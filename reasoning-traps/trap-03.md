# Critical Path Misdirection

**Domain:** Project Management

---

## 🧩 Prompt
Given tasks A(3d), B(2d), and C(5d) with A and B leading into C, what is the project duration?

---

## ✅ Correct Answer
8 days

---

## 🪤 Why It Stumps AI Models
Models may incorrectly add A, B, and C sequentially without analyzing parallel paths.
